STEP-BY-STEP DEMO FLOWS (build 6)

1) TERMS OF SERVICE / EULA (Guideline 1.2)
   a. Log out if already signed in: Profile tab → Settings (gear) → Log out.
   b. On the login screen, tap "Create account" / Sign up.
   c. Observe the required checkbox: "I agree to the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines…"
   d. Tap "Terms of Service" or "Community Guidelines" — each opens bookmarked.online/terms in Safari.
   e. The Sign up button stays disabled until the checkbox is checked.
   f. (Optional) Complete sign-up to verify the gate; or use the demo account above to skip to moderation flows.

2) PROFANITY FILTER (Guideline 1.2)
   a. Sign in with the demo account.
   b. Feed tab → tap the compose (+) button (or Home → share a thought).
   c. Type an obvious profane word in the post body and tap Post.
   d. An alert blocks posting: "Please remove profanity before posting."
   e. Feed tab → scroll to any post with flagged language — text is blurred with a frosted overlay until tapped.

3) REPORT CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
   a. Feed tab → find any post from another user.
   b. Tap the ⋯ (more) button on the post card.
   c. Tap "Report content".
   d. Select a reason (e.g. "Harassment or hate").
   e. Confirm — alert: "Report submitted. We review reports within 24 hours."
   f. Reports are stored in our admin queue (content_reports) for action within 24 hours.

4) BLOCK USER (Guideline 1.2)
   a. Feed tab → tap a post author's avatar or @username to open their profile.
      — OR open Messages, tap a conversation, long-press a message → Report (same moderation path).
   b. On the reader profile, tap ⋯ (top right).
   c. Tap "Block [username]".
   d. Confirm Block — their content is removed from your feed immediately; an auto-report is filed for developer review.

5) ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
   IMPORTANT: Do NOT delete the demo account (appreview@bookmarked.online) — it is shared for review.
   a. Create a temporary test account: Sign up → accept terms checkbox → set a username (e.g. reviewdelete2026).
   b. Profile tab → Settings (gear, top right).
   c. Scroll to the red "Delete account" section → tap "Delete my account".
   d. Type DELETE in the confirmation field → tap "Permanently delete account".
   e. Confirm in the system alert → account and all data are permanently removed; you return to the login screen.
   f. Account deletion is fully in-app; no email or website visit required.

MODERATION SUMMARY (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms + Community Guidelines: required checkbox on Sign Up; linked from login screen.
- Profanity filter: blocks offensive language on compose (posts, comments, messages) and blurs profanity in feeds.
- Report content: tap ⋯ on any feed post/review/discussion, long-press a message, or tap Report on a comment → choose a reason → stored in content_reports for admin review within 24 hours.
- Block user: available from the same menus and from any reader profile (⋯) → removes their content from your feed immediately and auto-files a report for developer review.
- Account deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete account → type DELETE → confirm.
