MODERATION FEATURES (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms + Community Guidelines: required checkbox on Sign Up; linked from login screen.
- Profanity filter: blocks offensive language on compose (posts, comments, messages) and blurs profanity in feeds.
- Report content: tap ⋯ on any feed post/review/discussion, long-press a message, or tap Report on a comment → choose a reason → stored in content_reports for admin review within 24h.
- Block user: available from the same menus and from any reader profile (⋯) → removes their content from your feed immediately and auto-files a report for developer review.
- Account deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete account → type DELETE → confirm.
